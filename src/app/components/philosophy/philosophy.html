Our Sacred Promise

You Shouldn’t Have to Question Your Food.

Every Jain in Bangalore knows the anxiety of asking: "Is this really Jain? Did they use the same oil? Is there onion in the gravy base?" We created Satvik Savour to eliminate that question forever.

"Our kitchen was founded with one unwavering vow: complete spiritual purity without sacrificing culinary craft. Our chefs prove every single day that rich gravies, slow-baked Marwari Dal Bati, and crisp Indo-Chinese noodles can be deeply satisfying without a single speck of onion, garlic, or root vegetables."
